📋 Android Landscape Diagnostic Test Procedure:

  1. Tap "Enable Motion Sensors" above (and allow permission if prompted).
  2. Rotate your phone to Landscape mode (earpiece on the left). Tap "Fullscreen / Landscape" if desired.
  3. Hold the phone comfortable and tap "Set Baseline / Zero".
  4. Test A (Tilt UP / DOWN): Pitch the top of the phone back towards you, then away. Note whether β (Beta) or γ (Gamma) changes and its sign (+ or -).
  5. Test B (Rotate LEFT / RIGHT): Turn your chair/torso left and right while holding the phone facing you. Note whether β (Beta) or α (Alpha) changes and its sign.
  6. Test C (Roll Clockwise / CCW): Steer the phone like a steering wheel. Note which value changes.
